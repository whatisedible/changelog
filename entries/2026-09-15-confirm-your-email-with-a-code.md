---
title: New accounts confirm their email with a code
topic: product
apps: [builder]
---
When you create a Menu Builder account, we email you a 6-digit code to confirm your address before you set up your business. It works the same way whether you sign up directly, after trying Edible with a photo of your menu, or from a demo link.

If you stop at the code step, log in later and you'll pick up on the same short business form.
