---
title: Put a menu live or hide it with one switch
topic: product
apps: [builder]
---
Every menu has a Live / Hidden switch, on your Menus page and in the editor. When a live menu has edits your guests can't see yet, a Publish changes button appears beside it.

On the Free plan, switching on a second menu offers to swap it with the one that's live. The other menu is hidden, not deleted.

You'll also see clearer confirmations when a menu goes live, is hidden or is updated, with a shortcut to reprint your dietary spreadsheet. The editor is easier to use on a phone too.
