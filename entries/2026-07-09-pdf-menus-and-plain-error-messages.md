title: PDF menus, and plain words when a scan fails
topic: product
apps: [builder, eat-hub]
---
You can now upload PDFs as well as photos: JPG, PNG or PDF, up to 5MB each.

When a menu can't be read, you get a plain explanation: the menu was unreadable, we're busy, the daily limit was reached, you're offline, or it timed out. No more technical errors or endless spinners, and a direct link to support if you need it.
