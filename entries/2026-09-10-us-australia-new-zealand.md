---
title: Edible for restaurants in the US, Australia and New Zealand
topic: product
apps: [builder, menu, eat-hub]
---
Restaurants in the United States, and in Australia and New Zealand, can sign up and choose their region. Their menus show the allergens that region's rules list: the nine named by the FDA in the US, and the 12 set by Food Standards Australia New Zealand, one standard covering both countries.

Diners can pick these regions in Eat Hub too, so a snapped menu is read against the allergen list that applies where they are. Allergen names also come in American and Australian English, for readers using those languages.
