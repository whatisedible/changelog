---
title: Swaps offered on request are read correctly from menu photos
topic: product
apps: [builder, eat-hub]
---
When a section of a menu carries a note like "gluten-free pasta available on request", every dish in that section shows gluten as something the kitchen can take out, with the note attached. Before, those dishes read as simply containing gluten.

"May contain" warnings are no longer offered as something to take out: a cross-contact risk isn't an ingredient the kitchen can remove.
