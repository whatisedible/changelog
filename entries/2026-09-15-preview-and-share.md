---
title: Preview and share your menus without leaving the page
topic: product
apps: [builder]
---
Preview opens over the page instead of in a new tab, so you can check your digital menu and your dietary spreadsheet without losing your place. You can print the spreadsheet from there too.

A new Share panel gathers every way guests can find your live menus: print the spreadsheet, copy your menu link, download a QR code, or copy the code for your website. It opens by itself when your first menu goes live.
