---
title: Know which allergens a menu actually tracks
topic: product
apps: [eat-hub]
---
Every snapped menu now comes with a coverage card: which allergens and diets the menu itself keeps track of, and which it doesn't. If a menu never mentions sesame, we say "ask" — not "safe".

The same rule applies to diets. A menu that doesn't track "vegan" won't quietly hide dishes from you; it tells you to check.
