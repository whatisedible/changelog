---
title: Crustaceans and molluscs are now told apart
topic: product
apps: [eat-hub, menu]
---
"Shellfish" used to be one bucket. Now a menu that names prawns is tracked as crustaceans and one that names mussels as molluscs — and tracking one never implies the other is covered. Where a menu only says "shellfish", we keep it general and tell you to ask.
