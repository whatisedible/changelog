---
title: Allergen names that follow each region's own rules
topic: product
apps: [menu, builder, eat-hub]
---
A menu only groups wheat, barley, rye and oats under gluten if the restaurant's regional standard names gluten. The US standard lists wheat on its own, so a US menu tagged with wheat reads "Wheat", not "Gluten (Wheat)", which is a claim the US rules don't make. Every other region we support uses the gluten grouping, so their labels are unchanged.

Two wording fixes reach every menu. Tree nut types are singular ("Almond", not "Almonds"), matching how a dish names an ingredient. Canadian menus read "Molluscs" rather than "Mollusks".
